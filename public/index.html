FAQ

Good questions.

Don’t see yours? Email info@suncoastservers.com.

Do you really handle “everything” IT?

Pretty much. Websites, hosting, computers, networks, cloud, email, security, social media ads, support desks, and automation are all in scope. If something is truly outside what we do, we’ll tell you right away and point you to someone good.

How does AI make you cheaper?

A big chunk of any IT job is research, repetitive setup, drafting, and sifting through logs. AI tools do that in minutes. Our people spend their time on the decisions and the finishing work, and we pass the savings on to you.

Is my data safe when you use AI?

We pick tools with your privacy in mind and keep a human in charge of every step. If you’d rather keep AI out of your project entirely, just say so and we will.

I’m not in Tampa. Can you still help?

Yes. Almost everything we do is remote, whether you are elsewhere in Florida, somewhere else in the United States, or outside the country.

Where is Suncoast Servers located?

Suncoast Servers LLC is based in Tampa, Florida. The office address is 5005 W Laurel St, Suite 100, Tampa, FL 33607. Most client work is remote.

Which parts of Florida do you serve?

We work with businesses across Tampa Bay, including Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Brandon, Wesley Chapel, Largo, Bradenton, and Sarasota, and with companies elsewhere in Florida, including Orlando, Miami, Jacksonville, Fort Myers, and Tallahassee.

Do you work with clients across the United States?

Yes. Companies anywhere in the United States can hire Suncoast Servers as a remote IT team. We work across U.S. time zones. International clients are welcome too.

What is the mobile device cloud?

It’s our own real-device lab: physical iPhones and Androids on U.S. carrier networks that you control remotely through your browser. It’s ideal for app testing and anything that needs a genuine U.S. mobile connection. Each phone is dedicated to one customer.

How much does it cost?

Community and customer support starts at $5 an hour. Websites, hosting, IT projects, cloud, security, ads, and automation are quoted before any work starts. The mobile device cloud starts at $149 a month per dedicated phone, and those plans typically run $149 to $199.
