## Questions

### Can you move us from Google to Microsoft, or the other way?

Yes. Ask with the number of mailboxes and what else lives in the suite, such as files and calendars. The quote depends on that, not on a slogan.

### Do you stay on as admin afterward?

We can. Some clients want the move and then they take the admin seat back. Either way is fine if it is agreed up front.

### Is this the $5 an hour service?

No. Workspace projects are quoted. The $5 rate is for customer and community support coverage.
