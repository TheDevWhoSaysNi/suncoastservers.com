Questions

Can you keep AI out of our data?

Yes. Say so at the start. We will also tell you if a tool we would normally use is a poor fit for your privacy needs.

Do you replace our staff?

No. The point is to take robot work off their plate: copying, sorting, answering the same question, moving a record from one app to another.

Is this the same AI that makes your other services cheaper?

Same idea, different place. Internally, AI shortens our labor. Here, we install something your team keeps using after we are done.
