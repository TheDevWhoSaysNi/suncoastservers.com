Questions

Do you only build brand-new sites?

No. We also take over sites that already exist: fixes, redesigns, speed, SEO, and moves onto hosting we can support.

Is hosting included?

It can be. Domains, SSL, and updates are part of keeping a site online after launch. The quote says what is included.

Can you help the site show up in search?

Yes, as part of the build or as a follow-up. That means clear pages, fast loading, titles that match the business, and copy about the services you actually sell. We will not promise a specific ranking.
