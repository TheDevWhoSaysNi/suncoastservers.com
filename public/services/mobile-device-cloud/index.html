Mobile device cloud

Real phones on real U.S. networks.

Suncoast Servers runs a mobile device cloud: physical iPhones and Android phones on real U.S. carrier networks, with secure browser access, from $149 a month per dedicated device.

Reserve a device → info@suncoastservers.com

What you get

  • A dedicated physical phone, never shared and never reused for another customer
  • Unlimited U.S. carrier data
  • One stable, real mobile IP, with no rotation
  • Secure remote access from a browser
  • Power, monitoring, and maintenance handled
  • Plans that typically run $149 to $199 per device each month

Emulators lie, and testing in production is a gamble. The lab is in our Tampa operation and gives you the real device. The team has more than 15,000 hours of device connectivity behind it, and the lab does not use emulators or shared devices.

Good fits

  • App QA on real hardware
  • Carrier and network testing
  • Location-sensitive features that need a genuine U.S. mobile connection
  • A consistent U.S. presence for a remote team
  • Ad and storefront previews on a real phone

Teams anywhere in the United States, and teams outside the country who need a U.S. handset, control the phone from a browser. The carrier network is American. The person clicking can be in Tampa, elsewhere in Florida, or on another continent.

Questions

Is the phone shared?

No. Each device is dedicated to one customer. It is not a pool, and it is not an emulator.

What does it cost?

From $149 a month per device. Plans typically run $149 to $199. The quote confirms the phone and the carrier setup.

Can this replace a U.S. office?

It can give you a real U.S. mobile connection and a stable mobile IP. It does not replace a street address, a bank account, or a person on site.
