# IT services for companies anywhere in Florida.

Suncoast Servers is one Tampa team for Florida businesses outside the immediate neighborhood: Orlando, Miami, Jacksonville, Fort Myers, Tallahassee, the Panhandle, and the smaller towns in between.

Suncoast Servers LLC is based in Tampa, Florida. Email info@suncoastservers.com. Office address: 5005 W Laurel St, Suite 100, Tampa, FL 33607, United States. Most work is remote. Primary service area: Tampa Bay, the rest of Florida, and the United States. International clients are welcome.

Canonical page: https://suncoastservers.com/locations/florida/

We do not have a Miami office or an Orlando office. Pretending otherwise would waste your time. Florida companies hire the Tampa crew remotely and get the same websites, hosting, help desk, cloud, security, ads, email, and AI work as a client on Westshore.

Most of Florida shares Eastern time with Tampa, so a call at 10 a.m. is 10 a.m. for both sides. The western Panhandle, including Pensacola, is on Central time. We already work across U.S. time zones, so that is a scheduling note, not a reason to say no.


## A few honest limits

- This is remote delivery. We will not promise a technician in every Florida city.
- There is one team. A busy week is a busy week, and the quote will say when we can start.
- The $5 an hour rate is customer and community coverage, not a blanket rate for engineering.
- Security work is a checkup, sign-in, device protection, and backups. It is not a 24-hour security operations center.

A clinic in Orlando, a store in Jacksonville, and a nonprofit in a small town can all open with the same email. If the work is outside what we do, the reply says that, and it says it quickly.
